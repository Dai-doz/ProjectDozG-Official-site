Bootloader Unlocking

Follow these core setup requirements strictly to ensure safety during the installation phase.

  1. 1Enable Developer options in settings.
  2. 2Plug the phone to the PC.
  3. 3Keep pressing the button of volume up and volume down.
  4. 4Now keep pressed up button to get in Bootloader Unlock Mode.
  5. 5Press vol + to unlock bootloader.

ROM Install Steps

Flashing instructions prepared for standard recovery steps.

  1. 1Make sure you're on the latest Stock firmware (Check on SamFW).
  2. 2Make sure your OEM Bootloader is Unlocked.
  3. 3Download OFOX/TWRP on Official site . Download Vbmeta.tar .
  4. 4Put device in Download Mode.
  5. 5Load OFOX/TWRP TAR file in AP, and under Odin Options, untick Auto Reboot.
  6. 6Force reboot by holding POWER + Vol Down.
  7. 7While on OFOX/TWRP, go to flash repartition.zip then reboot recovery and wipe all partitions.
  8. 8Put OFOX/TWRP in Sideload mode.
  9. 9Sideload the ROM from the computer.
  10. 10Before restart go to wipe and format data.
  11. 11Reboot and enjoy!
